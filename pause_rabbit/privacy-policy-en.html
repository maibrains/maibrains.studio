Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2025

maibrains studio ("Developer") operates PauseRabbit ("App"). This policy describes how we handle user information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration and does not collect personal information transmitted to any server. All settings and usage data are stored locally on the user's device only.

2. Accessibility Service Usage

The App uses the Android Accessibility Service to detect the package name of the currently running app in order to enforce user-configured blocking schedules. It does not collect screen content or user input. Detected information is not transmitted externally.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from the device.

5. User Rights

Users may uninstall the App at any time to delete all data, or request account and data deletion via email.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries: maibrains.studio@gmail.com
