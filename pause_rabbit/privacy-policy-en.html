2. Legal Bases for Processing

The Controller processes personal information under the applicable legal basis below.

  • Account authentication, core features, purchases, refunds and customer support: entering into and performing a contract with the user
  • Tax and e-commerce records and responses to legal requests: compliance with legal obligations
  • Marketing emails, personalized ads, and optional post-withdrawal contact: user consent
  • Security, incident analysis and non-personalized service improvement: the Controller’s legitimate interests where the processing is necessary, those interests clearly outweigh users’ rights, and the processing is substantially related to those interests and remains within reasonable bounds

Consent-based processing may be withdrawn, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Where advertising-identifier processing requires consent, it does not begin before optional consent is given. Where stability processing such as Crashlytics or Performance is based on legitimate interests, only the minimum technical information is processed, users may object, and consent is obtained if local law requires it.

A Google account identifier and email address are required to register, purchase information is required for paid features, and device identifiers are required to connect devices. Without this information, the relevant features cannot be provided. Marketing, optional advertising, and account-deletion feedback are optional, and refusing them does not disadvantage use of the core service.

Firebase Analytics helps identify operational issues and improve features by analyzing how permissions, sign-in, payments, ad rewards and pause features work, and usage trends for onboarding, missions and notifications. Collection settings and requests to suspend processing or delete information are described in Sections 13 and 10.

  • Meta App Events: Meta may retain event data for up to two years (Meta Business Tools Terms).

13. Automatic Collection Technologies and Choice Management

The app and external SDKs may use mobile-device identifiers, including app installation, session and advertising identifiers, and local device storage. Privacy-region information is used to block remote diagnostics collection and provide advertising privacy settings, and is deleted when app data is cleared or the app is removed.

Google’s advertising consent tool (UMP) displays choice screens according to the region and available messages.

Marketing consent may be changed through the app settings described in Section 9. Where Google provides privacy options, users may change advertising consent through the app’s advertising privacy settings. Device advertising privacy settings and Google settings also provide the advertising choices available in each setting. The app has no separate setting to opt into or stop Firebase Analytics, Performance or Crashlytics collection, and changing advertising settings does not stop that collection. Android permissions may be withdrawn in device settings, which may prevent related features from working. Requests to suspend processing or delete personal information follow Section 10.

Meta App Events (Facebook SDK) collects advertising identifiers and app install, app launch and conversion events and transmits them to Meta (Sections 7 and 8). Users may refuse or limit advertising-related collection and use as follows.

  • Android device settings: in the advertising section of settings (for example, Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Settings > Privacy & security > Ads personalization), delete the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads. The path may differ by device manufacturer and Android version.
  • Meta ad settings: manage ad preferences in Meta ad settings.

The app has no separate setting to opt into or stop Meta App Events transmission. If you delete the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads on the device, personalized linking through the advertising identifier is limited, but events such as app installs and launches, the Meta app anonymous identifier and the IP address may still be transmitted. Changing these settings does not limit your use of the app’s features.
