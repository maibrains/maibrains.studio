2. Legal Bases for Processing

The Controller processes personal information under the applicable legal basis below.

  • Account authentication, core features, purchases, refunds and customer support: entering into and performing a contract with the user
  • Tax and e-commerce records and responses to legal requests: compliance with legal obligations
  • Marketing emails, optional analytics and personalized ads, and optional post-withdrawal contact: user consent
  • Security, incident analysis and non-personalized service improvement: the Controller’s legitimate interests after giving priority to users’ rights
  • Protection of life or physical safety in an emergency, or another basis permitted by law

Consent-based processing may be withdrawn, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Where Firebase Analytics or advertising-identifier processing requires consent, it does not begin before optional consent is given. Where stability processing such as Crashlytics or Performance is based on legitimate interests, only the minimum technical information is processed, users may object, and consent is obtained if local law requires it.

A Google account identifier and email address are required to register, purchase information is required for paid features, and device identifiers are required to connect devices. Without this information, the relevant features cannot be provided. Marketing, optional analytics and advertising, and account-deletion feedback are optional, and refusing them does not disadvantage use of the core service.

4. Deletion of Personal Information

  1. When a retention period ends or a processing purpose is fulfilled, information is deleted so that it cannot be recovered or reproduced.
  2. Electronic files are securely deleted or made inaccessible.
  3. Information retained by law is logically or physically separated from active service information and access is restricted.
  4. Information remaining in backups is deleted according to the established backup-rotation cycle, and deletion status is reapplied after restoration.

5. Paid Tickets After Account Deletion

Valid paid tickets that are not automatically refunded when an account is deleted are retained separately from statutory transaction records for 5 years, together with the minimum identifiers needed to verify ownership, to fulfill the user’s property rights. If the user registers again with the same Google Account within 5 years, the remaining balance may be restored after verifying identity and the validity of the right. The information is deleted earlier if the tickets are refunded, the user waives the right or the right is lawfully extinguished. After 5 years, it is deleted or made irrecoverable unless there is another ground for retention, such as a legal dispute.
