Pause Rabbit Account and Data Deletion Guide

Effective date: August 15, 2026

This guide explains how to request deletion of a Pause Rabbit account and related data, which information is deleted, and which information is retained as required by law.

How Members Delete Their Accounts

  1. Open Pause Rabbit.
  2. In Settings, select Delete Membership under Account.
  3. Review the information displayed about subscriptions, refunds, remaining Tickets, and data deletion.
  4. Complete any required identity verification and final confirmation.

If the app cannot be accessed, email contact@maibrains.studio with the email address used for the account and a request for account deletion. The Operator may request the minimum necessary identity verification. Do not send a password or Google authentication token by email.

How to Delete Guest Data

Guest Service data, such as Pause schedules and notes, is generally stored on the device. Clear Pause Rabbit storage or app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete local Service data. The Operator cannot remotely restore or delete this data because it is not linked to a Member account. Technical data processed by external SDKs for advertising, analytics, and crash diagnostics is handled under the Privacy Policy and each external service’s settings.

Points to Review Before Account Deletion

  • For a subscription, the Operator processes cancellation of the next automatic renewal. Also confirm the cancellation status in Google Play subscription management.
  • Ticket refunds upon account deletion are calculated separately for each purchase. If all paid-value Tickets in a purchase are unused, a full refund is requested automatically. If some paid-value Tickets have been used, a partial refund is based on the ratio of unused paid-value Tickets to the initial paid-value quantity, and remaining bonus Tickets are revoked. Within the same purchase, paid-value Tickets are used first; no refund amount remains when only free bonus Tickets are left. The Operator processes partial refunds manually, which may take some time to complete.
  • Upon account deletion, a subscription refund is requested automatically when at least 24 hours of entitlement remain. If that condition is met, a full refund is requested when the purchase was made within 48 hours and no PREMIUM feature has been used. Otherwise, a prorated refund calculated by Google Play is requested. When a refund is requested, subscription entitlement and future renewal may end immediately. These criteria apply only to automated in-app refunds and do not limit statutory withdrawal or refund rights. See Article 9 of the Terms of Service for statutory withdrawal and refund details.
  • Account deletion proceeds even if a refund fails or has not been finalized. If automatic processing is not completed, the Operator handles follow-up settlement. Only the minimum information necessary for refund and Ticket verification and legal obligations is retained separately, and refunds are processed separately from account deletion. Regardless of automatic-refund eligibility, rights to request a refund under applicable law or Google Play policy remain available.

What Happens When an Account Is Deleted

After account deletion is completed, the following information is deleted from the Service’s operating environment or processed so that it can no longer be linked to the account:

  • Active Member status and access to the Service
  • Additional registered-device information and the contents of synchronized Service data
  • Free Tickets and related records
  • Future marketing-recipient status

The minimum identifiers needed for transaction records, refund processing, and pending ticket refund verification are retained separately from active Member information under the standards below.

Installed-app lists, paused apps, schedules, notes, and local state on the device can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Account deletion alone may not remove all local data remaining on the device.

The Google Account itself is not deleted. Management of the Google Account’s connection to the Service and permissions is available separately in Google Account settings.

Names and photos included in authentication information are removed upon account deletion. The email address needed for refund contact and authentication identifiers needed to identify the refund recipient are retained under the retention standards.

Contract, withdrawal, payment, and service-supply records are retained for the statutory 5-year period regardless of account deletion. The up-to-5-year period for account-deletion and security records is an operational retention ceiling; it does not extend the statutory 5-year obligation to all security records.

Retained information is separated from active Member information and used only for its stated purpose. If the same information is needed for two or more purposes stated in the Privacy Policy, the retention period for each purpose applies. It is deleted without delay when no legal retention obligation remains and each specified purpose has been fulfilled or its retention period has ended. The minimum information needed for ongoing subscriptions and unresolved refunds or settlement, and access, error, crash, and performance logs, follow the retention standards for their stated purposes in the Privacy Policy.

The account is removed from all future marketing-recipient lists immediately upon deletion. Selected deletion reasons, optional feedback, and follow-up consent may be withdrawn at contact@maibrains.studio and are processed only for their stated purposes during the periods in the table.

Outcome and Contact

After verifying the requester’s identity, the Operator notifies the requester of the outcome within the period required by applicable law. If some information cannot be deleted immediately because of a legal retention obligation, an ongoing payment or dispute, or protection of another person’s rights, the Operator explains the retained information, reason, and period.

For account deletion, retained-information, refund, or personal-information-rights inquiries, email contact@maibrains.studio.
