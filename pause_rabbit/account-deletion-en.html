Account and Data Deletion Request

Deleting App Data

All PauseRabbit data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app immediately removes all settings and usage records.

Deletion Request

If you would like to request additional data or account deletion, please contact us via the email below. Requests will be processed within 7 business days.

  • Email subject: [PauseRabbit] Account/Data Deletion Request
  • Include in body: your email address or identifiable information
Send Deletion Request Email

Scope of Deletion

The app does not store personal data on any server. Uninstalling the app completely removes all data from your device. In-app purchase history is managed by Google Play and can be canceled separately from the Google Play subscription management page.
